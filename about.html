About Me

Hello there, I'm Minh Nguyen, a student at UIT (University of Information Technology) currently shaping my path as a Backend Engineer and DevOps enthusiast. You are viewing my personal website, which serves as a digital notebook for documenting my technical journey, production workflows, and practical experiments.

Mainly, this blog is a dedicated space for posting about my work or breaking down complex computer networks, automation protocols, and system architectures—especially those challenging subjects that I find hard and need to review or consolidate again. I believe the best way to truly master a difficult concept is to explain it simply through writing.

You can contact me via email - quangminhanbu@gmail.com. For other ways of reaching me, have a look at the bottom left side bar.

I’m not a full-stack web developer, so I intentionally like to keep this site as simple as possible in terms of functionality and UI design. Instead of tweaking complex frontend layouts, I prefer spending my time under the hood—optimizing server logic, automating repetitive infrastructure tasks, and managing Linux systems. The Jekyll theme I’m using is Chirpy running on the GitHub Pages platform, which perfectly aligns with my preference for clean, minimalist, robust, and markdown-driven environments.

Beyond building APIs and configuring deployment pipelines, I have a strong interest in exploring how large-scale networks operate, experimenting with local environments, and turning old hardware into functional home servers. When I'm away from the terminal, I usually enjoy capturing everyday moments through mobile photography or diving into deep hardware specifications.
